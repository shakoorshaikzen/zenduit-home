## Platform

web

## Users

Fleet managers (hands-on, roughly 20–500 vehicles), operations directors (multi-site,
consolidation-minded), and safety/compliance leads. Practical, ROI-driven, non-technical.
**Confirmed 2026-08-04:** the homepage keeps a balanced address across all three personas —
no single one leads when trade-offs arise.

## Product Purpose

One platform to run an entire fleet: GPS fleet telematics, AI dash cameras & video safety,
asset tracking & monitoring, smart fleet sensors & alerts, routing & dispatch, fuel
management, maintenance, and ELD compliance. It exists so operators see everything live and
act before problems get expensive — "Measure · Monitor · Manage" (Zenduit's real tagline
framing, verified on zenduit.com). Homepage success = demo bookings: primary CTA
"Get a Demo", secondary "Check Our Prices".

## Positioning

**Confirmed 2026-08-04 — all four legs hold together; competitors can copy one, not all:**

1. **Open Geotab ecosystem** — integration marketplace; buyers keep existing hardware and
   data flows instead of being locked in.
2. **True single-platform breadth** — cameras, sensors, ELD, dispatch, maintenance genuinely
   in one login.
3. **Hands-on service & custom builds** — white-glove onboarding and per-fleet custom
   solutions the big players don't offer.
4. **Value for mid-size fleets** — enterprise-grade tooling packaged for fleets the big
   players overlook.

## Operating Context

Ops desk with a wall monitor; dispatchers keep the live map open all day. Buyers evaluate
against Samsara and Motive. Zenduit operates in the Geotab partner ecosystem. Real product
names (verified zenduit.com 2026-08-04): ZenCam Plus, 360° Fleet Visibility, ZenDoor, ZenID,
ZenTemp, ZenTitan, ZenTurbo, ZenduELD (current launch), ZenduConnect; vehicle trackers in
OBD, power, long-life, compact, heavy, and solar variants.

## Product Principles

1. Show the product working before claiming anything — the live console outranks imagery.
2. Every number on the page is real or absent; nothing is invented.
3. Mono voice for data, quiet neutrals for chrome — color only where it acts.
4. One platform is the story; each product section is proof of it.
5. Copy names concrete outcomes a competitor could not claim verbatim.
